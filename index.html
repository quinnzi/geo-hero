Fun Fact

Why did you make this? Okay Okay, so it's a long story made by many many many years of not being properly educated about the landforms on Earth. I sort of just assued everyone doesn't know where everything is, but that's simply not true. I asked my mother if she knew that Jamaica was not in Africa and she said "Of course I did??". So, um, my ignorance is not universal. Still, I know there's people like me out there. I once stumbled upon a post where everyone had no clue india was in Asia. Those people right there, are who this tiny project is for :)
How does this work? It spins a globe and basically tells you stuff about whatever country you land on. When it comes out, you can click the quiz icon at the top right the quiz yourself on how much you remember about the country. You can also see all the countries you've landed on by pressing the history icon :). The icon look like a clock!
Why make this project? As forementioned, I am personally terrible at anything Geography. My knowledge of countries is extremely scattered. I honest to God could not tell you where on earth Bangladesh is.
But what if I can't remember anything about the country after I spin? There's a quiz mode where you can fill in the blanks of what you just read! Just click on the question mark icon encased in a square!
What is that icon with the question mark?? It quizzes you on the country that you're currently on! When you get something wrong it will highlight in red or green :)
Is this project finished? If not, when will it be finished? I still want to add a news feature and refine the quizzing, but for now I'm pretty happy w it. I think that this could be a really addicting tool if done right. I could add a points system, leaderboards, etc etc. Just think about it. Wouldn't you want to have the highest country quiz rate on a leaderboard? I also want to maybe add a setting where you land on a country and have to get its name w/o a label.
