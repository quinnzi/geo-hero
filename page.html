Why did you make this? Okay Okay, so it's a long story made by many many many years of not being properly educated about the landforms on Earth. I asked my mother if she knew that Jamaica was not in in "Africa" and she said "Of course I did??" so, my ignorance is not universal. Still, there must be people like me who know little about this small tiny universe rock, and this is for you guys!
How does this work? It spins a globe and basically tells you stuff about whatever country you land on
Why make this? Well, I am personally terrible at anything Geography. My knowledge of countries is extremely scattered and I honest to god could not tell you where Bangladesh is
But what if I can't remember anything about the country after I spin? There's a quiz mode where you can fill in the blanks of what you just read!
What is the 'Collection' all about? It quizzes you on the countries that you've landed on before!
