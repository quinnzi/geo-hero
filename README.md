Hi There! This is my fun, tiny project I made. You can use it by downloading the folder & opening page.html OR you can visit the site at https://geo-hero.onrender.com or https://geo-spin.netlify.app!

What does it do?
Just click on the spin button and it will spin the globe to a random country, and that country will be in the center of the globe. I haven't figured out how to make the countries labeled after you spin just yet, buttt I've added a fe new things!
- It uses an api to get fun facts about countries now! Though the fun facts are scarce I still think its fun
- There's a quiz mode that is in its beta testing stage but it mostly works
- All of the fields are filled when you spin a country now!
- There's also a history page. The country counter is broken, buttt you can see all the countries you landed on!
